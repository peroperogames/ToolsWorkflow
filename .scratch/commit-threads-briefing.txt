The model could not know. A review run gets the new commits and the tail of the
session — `conversation`, which does carry the threads, is never passed to it — and
anything older than the handed history was invisible. `threads_briefing` now renders
the threads already on the pull request (path, line, open or resolved, the opening
160 characters) and `build_review_turns` puts them in the turn. The instruction is
deliberate about the one exception: a resolved thread is done, unless the new commits
changed the answer or the resolution was wrong — and then the comment has to say
what the evidence is. Suppressing every re-raise would also suppress correcting a
wrong one, which is how `README.md:27` was legitimately re-raised at 12:59:12.

The settle step stays as the backstop.
