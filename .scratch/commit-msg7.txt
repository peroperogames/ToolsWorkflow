fix: a PeroNPM-only release collects and uploads no artifact

Nothing reads the packages artifact unless the `external` job publishes to npmjs or
nuget: the tarball has already gone to PeroNPM from the package directory, and the
second runner cannot see this one's disk, which is the whole reason the artifact
exists. So `Collect the packages` and `Upload the packages` are gated on
`DO_NPMJS || DO_NUGET`, and the upload is fatal when they run — without those files
`external` has nothing to publish.

That is also the honest version of 12a28c7: making the upload continue-on-error let a
PeroNPM-only release skip the failure but still upload the files. ResourcesManager was
uploading a tarball that nothing would ever download.
