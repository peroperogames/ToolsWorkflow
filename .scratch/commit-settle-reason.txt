feat: settle says why a thread stayed open, and therefore why it did not approve

The comment was `未解决线程 1 → 1，暂不 approve。` — a fact with no reason, which is
exactly the question it raises. NativeAudio PR #1 is the case: one thread on
`AndroidProject~/.project`, `Resolved 0/1`, and the reader is left to guess whether
the decider ran, saw the code, disagreed, or failed.

The decider is now asked for the second half of its answer, a sentence per thread it
does not close —

    {"resolved": [...], "open": [{"id": "...", "reason": "<what is still missing>"}]}

— and that reason reaches the comment:

    未解决线程 1 → 1，暂不 approve：
    - `AndroidProject~/.project:12` — 新提交没有改动这个文件，构建产物仍然会被提交。

`decide_resolved_threads` and `settle_threads_and_approve` return a `SettleOutcome`
(resolved count, the open threads with their reasons, whether the approval was
submitted, whether the judgment happened at all) instead of a number and a bool, so
the command can tell the four states apart: resolved and approved, resolved but the
approval was not this run's to make, still open with reasons, and judgment failed.
The job log prints the same reasons, one line per thread.

Falls back honestly: a thread whose decider gave no sentence is shown with its own
opening comment and said to be that; more than five open threads are counted rather
than listed; a resolve the API refused is reported with the API's message.

Checked locally: the four states, the reason list, the no-reason fallback, the cap at
five, the unreadable-thread-state case and the dry run.
