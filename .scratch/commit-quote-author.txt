was left unchanged, stop — and what not to: no restating the warning, no verdict of
"false positive", and no [作者认为…] in front of a technical claim the author did not
make. The two notes above are the example in the prompt.

be null — `IEqualityComparer<object>.GetHashCode(object o)` is a non-nullable parameter.

should say "the author dismissed it" and not explain it for them.
