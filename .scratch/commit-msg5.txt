fix: a failed step before the review must not skip the review

RhythmAce-Neo #89 (run 41014711782): the artifact upload was refused with "Artifact
storage quota has been hit", and the log reads

  ##[end-action display=Review and settle the threads;outcome=skipped

The review step was gated on the steps before it succeeding — the implicit
success() — so one refused upload cost the whole review, after the build, the tests
and the static analysis had already run. It is `!cancelled() && not closed` now:
nothing but a cancelled run skips the review. The job still ends failed when an
earlier step did, so this hides nothing.

The upload itself is continue-on-error (0f580d4), so the quota cannot fail the job
in the first place; this covers every other way a step before the review can fail.
`check_action_conditions.py` holds both.
